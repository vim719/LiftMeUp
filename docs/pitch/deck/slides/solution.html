The solution

Type an address. Get a ranked ledger.

Auto-fill from the city

The address becomes a building ID, then its elevators and last filing dates load from NYC Open Data.

Live due dates

Annual checks are due one year after the last filing, five-year checks after five. Edit a date and the ranking updates.

Money at risk

An open-violation badge on each elevator and an unpaid fine total for each building.

AI briefing

One click writes a short summary of what is late, what is next and what it may cost.
