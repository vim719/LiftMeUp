Explained like you’re five

A traffic light for elevators

Buildings have elevators

A property manager looks after lots of them.

Each one must be checked

Every year, with a bigger check every five years. The city must be told.

Forget, and you pay

Thousands of dollars per elevator, and more every month it stays late.

LiftMeUp is the traffic light

Red is late, yellow is soon, green is fine. Fix red first.

Type in a building’s address. LiftMeUp asks the city’s public records what elevators are there, then tells you which are late.
